# ✨ AetherFX & Achievements

Every selection and completion has theatrical feedback anchored to its actual row or game card, not to an unrelated screen coordinate.

**Finishers:** sword slash, shattered glass fragments, dragonfire, frost shatter, arrow hit, lightning arc, critical impact and fireworks. Effect intensity, sound and motion preferences are configurable. Reduced-motion settings are respected and the particle canvas stops doing work when idle.

**Selection:** rune ring, light trail, hover sparks and target glow that move with the selected item.

**Chronicle:** persistent per-profile ranked achievements for verified MO2 upgrades, including *First Blood*, *Apprentice Smith*, *Veteran of the Forge*, *Master Enchanter*, *Dragonborn*, and *Legendary Smith*.

**World-Eater celebration:** special full-profile final victory animation gated behind a fresh complete MO2 audit; it can be safely previewed without issuing ranked rewards.
