# 🌲 Shiverfell Chronicles

Shiverfell is the separate-tab, original, top-down survival RPG included with every Modhunter launch option. It has a persistent wilderness, resource gathering, hunger/heat/stamina needs, campfire crafting, combat, wolf companions, player leveling, and save/resume.

The **Dawnshard** campaign leads through three discoverable landmarks to the Ashen Warden world boss. Two roguelite expeditions provide additional challenge: **Frostbound Rift** and **Wilderness Ironman**.

The survival game uses **Forge Sigils** for optional items only when linked to a live MO2 session. In offline practice it uses separate local practice credits and never claims actual mod updates. Updating a mod remains an intentional action in MO2, not a game side effect.
